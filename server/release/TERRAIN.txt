NEBO ATC/GCI - terrain height grids

Optional. A NEBO server samples each terrain once on its first mission (in the background, a
little each frame) and keeps the result in Saved Games\<server>\NEBO. These files are that
result for Caucasus, Syria and the Marianas at the default 500 m step (terrain_step in
NEBO.lua), so a server on those terrains starts with its terrain ready.

Install: copy the contents of copy-into-saved-games into the server's Saved Games folder
(for example Saved Games\DCS.server), so the files land in its NEBO folder.

The grids are sampled from DCS World. DCS World, its terrains and their data belong to Eagle
Dynamics SA and the terrain developers. They are not part of NEBO's GPL source and are not
code-signed.
