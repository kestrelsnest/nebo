NEBO client (for controllers)
=====================================
Operations manual: https://github.com/kestrelsnest/nebo/blob/main/docs/MANUAL.md

1. Install the .NET 10 Desktop Runtime (x64) once, from Microsoft:
     https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0   ("Desktop Runtime", Windows x64)
2. Put Nebo.Client.exe anywhere (e.g. a NEBO folder on the desktop) and start it.
3. Connection -> Connect -> New: a name, the server's address, its NEBO port and SRS
   port, the server password and your SRS EAM password (all from the server admin). Connect
   saves it (passwords encrypted for your Windows user only); next time pick it and Connect.
4. Servers with Discord login: keep the Discord app running and click Authorize in its popup.
   Then choose Blue or Red.

Optional
- A DCS install on the same PC gives the DCS map, DCS satellite imagery, airfield pavement,
  taxiway signs and navaids on the ATC diagrams. Without it, NEBO still works with its own maps.
  On the first start NEBO looks for DCS itself (ED installer, Steam, the usual folders on every
  disk) and asks you to confirm or pick the folder; change it later in Window -> Map data ->
  DCS World folder.
- SRS: with the EAM password saved, NEBO starts your SRS client and connects it in External
  AWACS Mode itself (untick that in the SRS tab to do it by hand); the SRS tab then sets your
  radios, shows who is on which frequency and who is talking.
- Window -> Theme (dark/light); GCI, ATC and DTC tabs can be detached onto a second screen.
- DTC tab: build data cartridges (F/A-18C, F-16C, F-14B(U)) and push them to your pilots; they
  need the DTC hook from the server admin. See the manual, section 12.
- Missile launch alarm: two short alarms when an enemy missile is fired at one of your players;
  Space centres the map on the target. Uses the PC's default sound output; switch it off or
  change its volume in Window -> Launch alarm.

Settings and layouts live in %AppData%\NEBO.
